SUGGESTED COMMIT MESSAGE

feat: add Synthetic Dataset V1 with evaluation harness and fix three pipeline defects

Adds a controlled, reproducible synthetic dataset generator (BPSK/QPSK/8PSK/BFSK/
16QAM/64QAM, configurable rates and SNR sweep, AWGN only, full ground truth) plus a
harness that scores the pipeline against it and reports unavailable metrics honestly.

Baseline measurement exposed three defects, each fixed with a minimal change and
regression tests:
- modulation_inference: long captures were decimated 256x with no anti-alias filter;
  now windowed at the native rate, and scored over 4 evenly spaced windows whose
  softmax vectors are averaged. CNN top-1 on V1 0% -> 73%, 16QAM 0% -> 80%.
- parameter_estimation: the envelope-squaring feature has an exact null at the symbol
  rate for rectangular pulses; now selects among three features by confidence.
  Symbol rate within 1% 0% -> 96.7%; BPSK/QPSK reach 0.000 BER at high SNR.
- qam_demod: sliced against an unnormalised +/-1/+/-3 grid while the pipeline delivers
  unit-RMS symbols; now scale-invariant. 16QAM/64QAM 0.000 BER when correctly routed.

Together these take median strict BER on V1 from 0.4944 to 0.0293, with 16QAM reaching
0.000 BER at 20 dB. Multi-window inference also raises fusion rejection 0.133 -> 0.200,
costing three low-SNR captures that previously produced chance-level BER under a wrong
label; fusion itself was not modified.

Tests: 358 passed. The single failure (test_reed_solomon_round_trip) is pre-existing
and environmental - reedsolo is not installed.

Known remaining issue: BFSK still classifies at 0% and demodulates at ~0.51 BER. Not
addressed here.
