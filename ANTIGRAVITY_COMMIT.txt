SUGGESTED COMMIT MESSAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

feat: add AM-DSB, AM-SSB and WBFM synthetic generation with analog harness safety

Adds analog synthetic capture generation in a registry deliberately separate from
the digital MODULATIONS registry, so the three call sites that default to
tuple(MODULATIONS) do not silently widen. AM-DSB uses a retained carrier
(1 + m*s(t)); AM-SSB uses the analytic signal, with USB as the documented default
and LSB configurable, recorded per capture in ground truth. WBFM integrates the
message phase explicitly - phi[n] = (2*pi/Fs) * cumsum(f_c + df*s[k]) - giving a
constant-envelope waveform whose configured peak deviation is reproduced exactly.

Analog captures carry no bits, no symbol rate and no Es/N0. Two fixes make the
evaluation harness handle that honestly rather than crashing or inventing a metric:
load_ground_truth returns None for a null bits block, and the harness forces
ber_status="unavailable" with reason "analog_no_transmitted_bits" even when
dispatch synthesises threshold bits.

Independent oracles validate generation only - not demodulation. AM-SSB
unwanted-sideband suppression measures 140-176 dB per tone against a 30 dB target.
WBFM peak deviation measures 15000.000 Hz against 15000.0 configured, with noiseless
message-recovery correlation of 1.000000 and monotone degradation under AWGN.

Known limitation, not addressed here: the CNN has no analog class, so analog
captures never reach demodulate_am / demodulate_ssb. AM-DSB is labelled PAM4 and
WBFM is labelled BPSK, which routes WBFM into the 2PSK demodulator; the guard
correctly refuses to score the resulting bits. No classifier, fusion threshold or
dispatch route was changed to hide this. Entry 026 adds a narrow fusion fallback
that recovers an analog label from the CNN's own ranked alternatives when a digital
top-1 is already rejected and the classical family is analog-like; measured benefit
is 1 firing in 15 analog captures, and 0 of 40 frozen V1 captures change.
Entry 027 then gives the classical detector a positive analog test (frequency_cv
below 0.9 with no fourth-power line) in place of decision-by-elimination: 0 of 800
digital controls become analog-like and no digital capture changes family, while
AM-DSB at a non-zero carrier offset now routes to demodulate_am in 4 of 5 seeds.
Entry 028 then makes analog dispatch symbol-rate independent: analog no longer
requires or uses a symbol rate, and no longer decimates by one. WBFM message
recovery through dispatch goes 0.145 -> 1.000, AM-DSB message tones stop aliasing,
and AM-DSB at a non-zero carrier offset recovers 0.94 correlation end to end.
Digital dispatch, including both timing-offset heuristics, is unchanged.
Entry 030 closes the SSB carrier gap at the ingestion layer: a SigMF .sigmf-meta
sidecar now populates metadata["center_frequency_hz"], which estimate_parameters
already preferred and preprocess already preserved. SSB recovers at 0.995-1.000
with a sidecar and is unchanged without one. The synthetic generator writes no
sidecars, so ground truth can never reach production ingestion.

Tests: 646 passed (test_model_report.py ignored - h5py absent). The single failure,
test_reed_solomon_round_trip, is pre-existing and environmental.
